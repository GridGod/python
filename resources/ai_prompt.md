## **10. How to use AI as your programming tutor**

You should use AI as a personal instructor, not as a code generator that does all your work.

Whenever you face a problem, follow this sequence:

1. Attempt the problem yourself for 10–15 minutes.
2. Identify exactly where you are stuck.
3. Ask AI to explain the underlying concept.
4. Request a hint rather than the complete solution.
5. Write the solution yourself.
6. Ask AI to review your code.
7. Test the code and explain it in your own words.

Use this prompt:

Act as my senior software engineering instructor and programming mentor.

I am following a structured 45-day Python learning programme.

Today's topic: [INSERT TOPIC]

My current assignment: [INSERT ASSIGNMENT]

Teaching instructions:

1. Explain the concept from first principles.
2. Explain the syntax and terminology.
3. Give me simple examples.
4. Give me three progressively difficult exercises.
5. Do not reveal the complete solution immediately.
6. Give hints when I get stuck.
7. Review my code for bugs, efficiency, readability and best practices.
8. Explain why an error occurs rather than just correcting it.
9. Ask me questions to verify my understanding.
10. Evaluate whether I am ready to proceed to the next topic.

My objective is to become an independent programmer capable of designing and developing complex software, applications, games and AI systems.

Teach me to think like an engineer, not merely copy code.
